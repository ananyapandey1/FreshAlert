Privacy Policy for FreshAlert

Effective Date: July 28, 2026

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

Welcome to FreshAlert ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data collected through the FreshAlert web application. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect your information, with special emphasis on Google User Data accessed via Google OAuth APIs.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential information needed to provide intelligent food tracking and expiry reminder services:

  • Account Credentials: Email address and encrypted password hash created during registration.
  • Kitchen Inventory Data: Product names, added dates, calculated expiry dates, and uploaded product image thumbnails or receipts.
  • Google Account Data (OAuth): When you voluntarily connect Google Calendar, we store OAuth authorization and refresh tokens to communicate with the Google Calendar API on your behalf.

2. Google User Data Access & Usage Disclosures

FreshAlert requests access to the following Google OAuth API scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events (Google Calendar API - View and edit events on your calendars)

How we use Google User Data:

  • We use this permission solely to create, update, and manage calendar event reminders on your primary Google Calendar when a food item approaches its expiration date (7-day advance warning) or reaches its expiry date.
  • We interact exclusively with events created by FreshAlert. We do not read, modify, copy, or delete any of your non-FreshAlert calendar events or personal calendar schedules.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
FreshAlert's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Data Protection, Sharing & Non-Disclosure Guarantees

We enforce strict data protection practices regarding your personal and Google user data:

  • No Data Sale: We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data or Google user data to third parties under any circumstances.
  • No Advertising Use: Your Google user data is never used for targeting advertisements, market research, or lead generation.
  • No AI Model Training: We do NOT use your Google user data or calendar entries to train or refine generalized AI, machine learning, or LLM models.
  • No Unauthorized Third-Party Transfer: We do not transfer Google user data to third parties unless required by applicable law.

4. Data Storage & Security Measures

Your authorization tokens and account details are protected using modern security standards:

  • All Google OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest in our database using industry-standard cryptography.
  • All data transmissions between your browser and our servers take place over secure HTTPS / TLS channels.
  • Access controls restrict database access strictly to automated server routines required to manage your reminders.

5. Data Retention, Unlinking & Account Deletion

You retain full control over your data at all times:

  • Unlinking Google Calendar: You can disconnect Google Calendar anytime in the Settings screen within the app. Upon unlinking, your Google tokens are immediately removed from our database.
  • Account Deletion: You can delete your account permanently via Settings -> Delete Account. Account deletion immediately and permanently wipes all your kitchen inventory items, account records, and stored Google OAuth tokens.
  • Google Permissions Manager: You can also revoke FreshAlert's access directly via your Google Account Permissions Manager.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
